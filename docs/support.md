# RecoLing Support

RecoLing is an iOS app that turns photos of your handwritten language-lesson notes into flashcards. Take a photo of your notes, review the vocabulary pairs it finds, and study them with spaced repetition. Your decks and review history stay on your device.

## Contact

Questions, bug reports or feedback: [maksym.ked@gmail.com](mailto:maksym.ked@gmail.com)

When reporting a problem, please include your iOS version, the app version (shown in the App Store) and a short description of what happened.

## Common questions

**Does RecoLing need an account?**
No. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

**Where are my cards stored?**
Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them, so export any decks you want to keep (CSV export) before uninstalling.

**What happens to the photos I import?**
Each photo is sent to our server and on to Anthropic's Claude AI, which extracts the vocabulary. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) for details.
